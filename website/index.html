How it works

Find. Choose. Pay. Track.

Four steps, one task, and everyone sees the same thing.

  1. FindSend a photo, a link or a few words. The assistant reads it and only asks what it needs to know.
  2. ChooseA short list with delivered prices in shillings and an arrival date. Anything that doesn’t match moves out of the way.
  3. PayWhoever is paying sees the exact item, seller and total, and approves it once in PayPal.
  4. TrackEveryone sees the same receipt, then updates until it’s ready to collect.

Options come with plain reasons. Where a size or a shipping cost isn’t known yet, it says so instead of guessing.

Walkthrough

Try it yourself

The quickest way is WhatsApp. For the whole journey, with Mum choosing and you paying, follow the steps in the web app. It takes about ten minutes.

On WhatsApp

Your first message is already typed. Press send and My Plug explains how it works.

In the web app

You play two people: the one shopping, and Mum. You’ll need:

  • Two browser windows that don’t share a sign-in: a normal one for you, a private one for Mum.
  • A PayPal Sandbox personal buyer login. It’s a test account, so no real money moves.
  • A photo of what you want. No photo? The app has a sample carrier drawing to download.
Open the web app

Open it in your normal window, then follow the steps. The code is on GitHub.

  1. YouUpload the photo

    Click Upload photo, add a note such as “For Mum. It must open from the top”, then Start with this photo.

  2. YouCheck what it understood

    The assistant lists what it heard, like “opening: top”, with the words it relied on. Nothing applies until someone confirms.

  3. YouSend it to Mum

    Open Share, choose Send to Mum to choose, and copy the link.

  4. MumSay what matters and confirm

    Paste the link into the private window. Open Ask or change something, type “It must open from the top”, then Confirm. Mismatches move to Doesn’t match.

  5. MumAsk a question, then choose

    Ask anything, like “Which suits a small cat?”, then tap Choose this one.

  6. YouReview and pay

    Reload your window. It moves to Pay with the exact item and total. Tap Pay with PayPal (sandbox), sign in with the sandbox buyer and approve.

    Cancel at PayPal once if you like: there’s no receipt, and you can pay again.

  7. BothSee the receipt

    Both windows show “Paid” and the same receipt. Mum sees it without any payment buttons.
